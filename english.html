Part A: Reading Comprehension

The Helpful Little Girl

Ada was a little girl who was kind and bright.

She helped her mother from morning till night.

Every day, she made her bed and kept her room neat.

She watered flowers and pulled out weeds beneath her feet.

One sunny day, she saw an old woman walking down the street.

The woman carried oranges in a basket, but she moved her tired feet.

Ada ran to help her and carried the basket with care.

The woman smiled and thanked Ada for being kind and fair.

She gave Ada three oranges, all juicy and sweet.

Ada shared them with her brother, who thought they were a treat!

Their mother smiled proudly and said, “You have done what is right.

Kindness makes the world a better place and fills our hearts with light.”
