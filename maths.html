🔟 A. Number bonds to 10

What does it mean?
Number bonds are two numbers that fit together to make a total. Think of 10 as a full ten-frame: if 4 counters are there, 6 more make 10.

🐞 B. Doubles and near doubles

What is a double?
A double means adding the same number twice. Near doubles use a number next to a double, so you can work it out quickly.
Example: double 5 = 5 + 5 = 10
Near double: 5 + 6 = 10 + 1 = 11

🌉 C. Bridge through 10

How does bridging work?
Break the second number into two parts. First, make 10; then add what is left. For 8 + 5, split 5 into 2 and 3. So 8 + 2 = 10, then 10 + 3 = 13.
Example: 8 + 5 = 8 + 2 + 3 = 13

🤖 D. Subtraction: count back

How do we take away?
Start at the first number and count backwards by the number you are taking away. For 17 − 5, start at 17 and jump back five steps: 16, 15, 14, 13, 12.
Example: 17 − 5 = 12

🧩 E. Find the missing number

Look at the whole sum.
The missing number is the number that makes the sum true. Think: “What number do I need to add or take away?”
Example: 11 − □ = 3. Count back from 11 to 3: 8 steps, so the answer is 8.
