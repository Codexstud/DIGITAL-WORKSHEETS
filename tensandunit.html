🌟 Mighty Maths: Adding Tens and Units 🌟

3rd Class • Addition with two-digit numbers

📘 Let's learn!

When we add two-digit numbers, we add the units first. Then we add the tens.

Worked example: 32 + 14

T
U
3
2
1
4
4
6
32 + 14 = 46
2 units + 4 units = 6 units
3 tens + 1 ten = 4 tens
1. Add the units
2 + 4 = 6. Write 6 in the Units column.
2. Add the tens
3 + 1 = 4. Write 4 in the Tens column.
3. Read the answer
4 tens and 6 units make 46.

Remember: Tens always go under T and units always go under U.
